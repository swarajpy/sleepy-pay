Paying

Steps (menu wording can differ slightly by bank)
  1. Tap Open *99# below. Your phone's dialer starts a USSD session.
  2. Pick Send Money, then UPI ID.
  3. Paste the UPI ID, enter the amount, and add a remark if asked.
  4. Enter your UPI PIN to confirm.
